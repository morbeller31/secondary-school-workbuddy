## Resource rules

- Teacher instructions and the assigned page override general assumptions.
- Verify edition, unit, exercise, and page before using an answer resource.
- Show answer material only after the learner attempts the task, unless a parent, caregiver, or teacher explicitly requests direct checking.
- Do not invent quotations, page numbers, rubrics, or answer-key wording.
- Keep legally obtained course materials in private storage and out of public Git repositories.
